What you get

Finds the blast for you

No campaign IDs, no workflow changes. It looks at what was actually sent and groups texts sent close together into blasts. Pick the one you mean.

Checks real reply history

A conversation that ends on your text isn't proof nobody answered. It reads each contact's messages and drops anyone with an inbound text, call, email or chat.

Strict or lenient

Leave out anyone who has ever replied, or only people who replied since this blast. Your call.

One tag, then resend in GHL

It adds a dated tag like no-reply-oct-14. Filter by it in Contacts, select all, send. Tagging twice is harmless.

Runs every morning if you want

Set a time and timezone per sub-account. Each blast is tagged once; a day with no new blast does nothing.

Preview and CSV

See who'll be tagged before you tag, filter by text, download the list.
