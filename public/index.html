Questions

Does it send any messages?

No. Blast Follow-Up only adds a tag. You send the resend yourself from HighLevel, so nothing goes out without you.

What if I run it twice?

Tagging is safe to repeat. Each blast is only tagged once by the schedule, and re-tagging a contact changes nothing.

Does it work for my whole agency?

Yes. Approve our app once for your agency and every sub-account is available, or connect a single sub-account with a Private Integration Token. One subscription covers all of them.

Is my data safe?

Access tokens are encrypted and only used server-side to make the calls you ask for. They're never sent to your browser. Disconnect a sub-account or uninstall the app from HighLevel at any time.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the Billing button on your dashboard. You keep access through the period you've paid for.
